title: "How to Delete an Enterprise Developer Portal Deployment in Tyk Cloud"
description: "The ticket addresses a customer inquiry regarding how to decommission an unused Enterprise Developer Portal (EDP) deployment in Tyk Cloud. Support provided step-by-step instructions for deleting the portal instance directly from the Tyk Cloud Console UI."
---

<h2>Overview</h2><p>This guide explains how to delete an Enterprise Developer Portal (EDP) deployment from Tyk Cloud when it is no longer needed.</p><h2>Prerequisites</h2><p>To delete an Enterprise Developer Portal deployment, your user account must have <strong>Organization Admin</strong> or <strong>Team Admin</strong> permissions for the organization or team managing the deployment.</p><h2>Steps to Delete an Enterprise Developer Portal</h2><ol><li>Log in to the Tyk Cloud Console and open the deployments page at <strong>https://dashboard.cloud.tyk.io/deployments</strong>.</li><li>In the deployments list, locate the <strong>Enterprise Developer Portals</strong> section.</li><li>Click on the name of the portal in the <strong>Developer Portal Name</strong> column to open its deployment overview.</li><li>In the top right navigation bar, click <strong>Edit Details</strong>.</li><li>Scroll to the bottom of the deployment details page.</li><li>Click <strong>Delete Enterprise Developer Portal</strong>.</li><li>Confirm the deletion when prompted. The associated portal instance, database, and configurations will be permanently decommissioned.</li></ol><h2>Verification</h2><p>Navigate back to the Tyk Cloud deployments list. Confirm that the portal no longer appears under Enterprise Developer Portals and that your organization portal deployment quota has been freed.</p><h2>Applies To</h2><ul><li>Tyk Cloud</li><li>Enterprise Developer Portal deployments</li></ul>
