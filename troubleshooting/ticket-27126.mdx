## Overview

This guide explains how to delete an Enterprise Developer Portal (EDP) deployment from Tyk Cloud when it is no longer needed.

## Prerequisites

To delete an Enterprise Developer Portal deployment, your user account must have **Organization Admin** or **Team Admin** permissions for the organization or team managing the deployment.

## Steps to Delete an Enterprise Developer Portal

1. Log in to the Tyk Cloud Console and open the deployments page at **https://dashboard.cloud.tyk.io/deployments**.
2. In the deployments list, locate the **Enterprise Developer Portals** section.
3. Click on the name of the portal in the **Developer Portal Name** column to open its deployment overview.
4. In the top right navigation bar, click **Edit Details**.
5. Scroll to the bottom of the deployment details page.
6. Click **Delete Enterprise Developer Portal**.
7. Confirm the deletion when prompted. The associated portal instance, database, and configurations will be permanently decommissioned.

## Verification

Navigate back to the Tyk Cloud deployments list. Confirm that the portal no longer appears under Enterprise Developer Portals and that your organization portal deployment quota has been freed.

## Applies To

- Tyk Cloud
- Enterprise Developer Portal deployments
