# 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy answers one question: **given a piece of content, where does it go?**

It governs:

- What content types exist and how a page is classified
- Where a page sits in navigation
- Where the file lives on disk and what it is called
- What metadata a page should declare

It does **not** govern voice, grammar, or formatting. Those belong to [`writing-style-and-conventions.md`](../writing-style-and-conventions.md). Where this policy and the writing style rules disagree, this policy wins on structure and the writing style rules win on prose.

## Status: Guidance, Not Enforcement

This policy describes the target state. The current docs do not follow it yet, and many existing pages break its rules.

- Use the rules as guidance for new content and as direction for improvements.
- The words "must", "never", and "required" in these files describe the target state. They are not merge conditions.
- Do not block a PR or refuse a placement because content does not follow the policy.
- Do not restructure, rename, or move unrelated pages to make them follow the policy. Make structural changes in dedicated PRs.
- If the policy conflicts with the conventions of nearby pages, keep the page consistent with its neighbours and mention the difference in the PR.
- No CI check enforces this policy.

The policy will change as the docs improve. If a rule does not fit real content, propose a change to the policy.
