# 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy answers one question: **given a piece of content, where does it go?**

It governs:

- What content types exist and how a page is classified
- Where a page sits in navigation
- Where the file lives on disk and what it is called
- What metadata a page must declare

It does **not** govern voice, grammar, or formatting. Those belong to [`writing-style-and-conventions.md`](../writing-style-and-conventions.md). Where this policy and the writing style rules disagree, this policy wins on structure and the writing style rules win on prose.
