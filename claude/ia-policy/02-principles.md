# 2. Principles

These resolve ties when the rules in the other policy files conflict or run out.

**P1 — Organise by reader intent, not by our org chart or component boundaries.** If a reader must know which Tyk component implements a feature in order to find its documentation, the IA has failed. The top-level test is *what am I operating on?*, not *which team built it?*

**P2 — Every page is a front door.** Assume most readers arrive from search, a support ticket, or an AI assistant, not from the homepage. Every page must orient the reader without relying on sidebar position.

**P3 — One canonical page per concept.** Related content links; it does not restate. Where text genuinely must appear twice, use a Mintlify snippet with a single source. Never maintain parallel copies.

**P4 — One page, one job.** A page has exactly one primary content type. Mixing concept, procedure, and reference on one page is the most common defect in the current corpus and the one this policy most wants to prevent.

**P5 — Show enough to choose, not everything at once.** Progressive disclosure at every level. Shallow, wide navigation beats deep, narrow navigation.

**P6 — Build bottom-up, read top-down.** Authors work upward from reference; readers arrive from the top. Facts live at the lowest layer that can own them, and every layer above links down to them rather than restating them.
